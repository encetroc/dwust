# Dwust UI

This context describes the player-facing HUD and its layout concepts.

## Language

**HUD placement grid**:
The 16-pixel alignment grid that constrains HUD panel positions and dimensions. Panel widths and heights are whole multiples of its spacing.

**HUD content grid**:
A row-and-column arrangement of content inside a HUD panel. A cell may contain a nested grid.

**Combat timeline item**:
A compact representation of one combat entity, with its name and health numbers above a health bar.
